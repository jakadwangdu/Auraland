Aura Island folds notifications, charging and music into one small pill. It rests until something matters, then opens softly.

Download APK Free for Android 8 and up

Three moments, and one exit.

A message arrives

The pill opens for a few seconds, shows who wrote and what they said, then folds away. Your notification stays untouched in the shade.

You plug in

Your battery fills in slowly and quietly. It never interrupts something that matters more.

Music plays

Whatever is playing becomes a small live shape. Open it for controls, fold it when you are done.

You turn the phone sideways

Watching a video or playing a game? The island disappears completely and returns when you rotate back.

A pill with a few tricks.

Quick menu

Tap the pill for a torch, scanner, camera and the apps you pick. One tap each.

Ask anything

Long-press to chat in English or Hinglish. Say "torch on" or "open YouTube" and it just happens.

Right on your camera

Nudge it left, right, up or down, or let it align itself to your camera cutout.

Install in a minute.

  1. Download the APKOpen it on your phone and allow installs from your browser when asked.
  2. Allow display over other appsThis lets the pill sit above whatever you are using.
  3. Turn on notification accessNeeded to read previews. Hide them any time in settings.
  4. Let it run in the backgroundOn Xiaomi and POCO, set Autostart on and battery saver to No restrictions.
